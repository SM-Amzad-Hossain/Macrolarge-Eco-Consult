BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A little more clarity.

All frequently asked questions
Do you get us ISO 14001 certified?

No. Certification is issued independently by an accredited certification body, not by us. What we do is help you design, implement, and prepare your environmental management system so it’s genuinely ready for that independent certification process.

Do you only take on large projects, or one-off assessments too?

Both. Many clients start with a single assessment, like a carbon footprint or an ISO 14001 gap analysis, and move into ongoing retainer support once priorities are clear. There’s no minimum commitment to get started.

What is a Life Cycle Assessment, and do we need one?

A Life Cycle Assessment measures the environmental impact of a specific product, from raw materials through manufacturing, use, and disposal. It’s most relevant if you manufacture physical products and need to back up sustainability claims, compare material choices, or respond to customer or retailer requirements.
