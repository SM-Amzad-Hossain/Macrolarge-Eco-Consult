Enquiry privacy notice for this static website preview.

Draft for client review before launch. This notice describes the current static website, not an approved production privacy policy.

What the enquiry form does

The form uses your name, work email, company, selected service and message to prepare an email draft on your device. It opens your email application using a mailto link. You must review the email and press Send yourself. The website does not send the email, upload your form details to a server or save them in browser storage.

When you choose to send an email

The information in your email will be handled by your email provider and the recipient’s email service. Macrolarge Eco Consult may use the information you send to respond to your enquiry. Please do not include confidential or sensitive personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This implementation does not set analytics or advertising cookies, use tracking pixels, or load third-party fonts. Fonts and imagery are served locally. When the website is hosted online, the hosting provider may process technical request logs to operate the service.

Questions about your information

For business privacy enquiries, contact info@ecoconsult.com or write to Macrolarge Eco Consult Ltd, First Floor Front Office, 14 Cloth Hall Street, Huddersfield, HD1 2DR.

Before production launch

The site owner must approve a full UK GDPR privacy notice confirming the data controller, lawful basis, retention period, email and hosting processors, any international transfers, individual rights and the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. If a form backend or analytics is added later, update this notice to reflect the actual implementation.
